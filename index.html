Make each review count.

Flashcards: click the card or press Space to flip between front and back. The star at the bottom saves a favorite (收藏).

Swipe review: swipe or drag right if you remembered; left if you forgot. You can also use the visible buttons or ← / → keys. A left swipe records the card and its concept in your wrong-question deck.

Test: choose an answer and select Check answer. Scoring is automatic. An incorrect answer saves your choice, the correct answer, and the knowledge point. Test questions have curated choices; the question count shows the available bank for your selection.

Wrong deck (错题本): Needs review shows current misses. All missed keeps the history even after you get something right. A successful review resolves the current flag; it does not erase the mistake history.

Scheduling: Forgot is due after 10 minutes. Remembered is due after 1, 3, 7, 14, then 30 days. These are self-ratings; tests are scored against an answer key.

CHEM 121 angle convention

The four supplied VSEPR tables are the source. Questions explicitly asking for a table angle use its approximate values—such as 109.5° for tetrahedral-derived shapes. These are not exact measured angles for every molecule. Diagrams are perspective projections, not drawings from which to measure angles.
