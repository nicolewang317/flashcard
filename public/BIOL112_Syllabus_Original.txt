# Unit 2 Overview - Macromolecules the building blocks of cells

## **Purpose of Unit 2:**

In order for cells to be self-replicating, cells need to make parts and have a container to keep everything together. This unit introduces you to the major macromolecules that are the building blocks of cellular structures, cellular function and biological information flow.  This includes their roles in cells, where they are found within bacterial and eukaryotic cells, their synthesis and assembly into higher order structures.  This module also introduces you to how materials are moved in and out of the cellular container.

### **Module 2-3: Making a container - lipids and membranes**

Purpose of the Module: This module builds on the modules on macromolecules and chemistry to explore the formation and function of biological membranes.  Membranes are a container for cellular contents and cellular compartments and function as a selective, semi-permeable barrier.  This module explores how membrane bilayers are formed; the types of proteins that are associated with membranes; how materials move across membranes and the model of membranes as a fluid mosaic.
