What does A represent in AXₓEₑ notation?	The central atom.
What does X count in the supplied VSEPR tables?	Atoms attached to the central atom.
What does E count in the supplied VSEPR tables?	Lone pairs on the central atom.
Which total selects the VSEPR parent shape?	E + X: lone pairs on A plus attached atoms.
Does the molecular shape name include the positions of lone pairs?	No. It describes the attached atoms; lone pairs still influence their arrangement.
When do the parent shape and molecular shape have the same name in these tables?	When E = 0: every parent position is occupied by an attached atom.
Why can two molecules with the same parent shape have different molecular shapes?	Different numbers or positions of lone pairs leave different arrangements of attached atoms.
In VSEPR domain counting, is a double bond one region or two around A?	One bonding region; multiple bonds connect A to one attached atom in the table’s X count.
E + X = 2. What is the parent shape?	Linear.
What E + X total gives a linear parent shape?	2.
E + X = 3. What is the parent shape?	Trigonal planar.
What E + X total gives a trigonal planar parent shape?	3.
E + X = 4. What is the parent shape?	Tetrahedral.
What E + X total gives a tetrahedral parent shape?	4.
E + X = 5. What is the parent shape?	Trigonal bipyramidal.
What E + X total gives a trigonal bipyramidal parent shape?	5.
E + X = 6. What is the parent shape?	Octahedral.
What E + X total gives a octahedral parent shape?	6.
E + X = 7. What is the parent shape?	Pentagonal bipyramidal.
What E + X total gives a pentagonal bipyramidal parent shape?	7.
In a perspective drawing, what does a solid wedge mean?	A bond projecting toward the viewer.
In a perspective drawing, what does a dashed wedge mean?	A bond projecting away from the viewer.
What do two dots beside A usually represent in these diagrams?	One lone pair on the central atom.
Does rotating a perspective drawing change the molecular shape?	No. The relative three-dimensional arrangement stays the same.
