## Boundaries and source fidelity

- Lecture 3 expands four main growth phases into seven; cards keep both contexts.
- Lecture 7 p. 5 gives the course-specific PD–PD hydrogen-bond convention.
- Lecture 6 p. 7 defers detailed thioester importance to BIOL 201; old extension cards remain marked.
- Amino-acid tables are provided on exams (L5 p.34; L7 p.19; L8 p.8; L10 p.24). Cards focus on structural reasoning, not memorizing all names.
- L9 p.40 says transporter names need not be memorized. Its small-intestine transporter subtype label is not made into a recall fact.
- L2 pp.22–23 has no definitive classification answer for the unusual-cell case. No forced species/classification answer was invented.
- L10 p.34 only fills the primary-structure row; the remaining blank rows are not presented as teacher-provided answers. Existing prereading folding cards retain their earlier source status.
- L10 p.33 uses a simplified residue explanation. The new card tests incorporation, not the incorrect absolute claim that no residue can donate a proton.
- Exact genome numbers are labelled as lecture examples, not universal or newly verified genome annotations.
- This is coverage of supplied lectures and earlier notes, not a claim that every syllabus objective has been taught. Allosteric regulation and later nucleic-acid material await further source material.
