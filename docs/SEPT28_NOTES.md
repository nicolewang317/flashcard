## Scientific clarifications

- A five-carbon pentose does not have five carbons in its five-membered ring: that ring contains four carbons and one oxygen.
- The phosphate illustration is an organic phosphate monoester, not free PO₄³⁻ at physiological pH. Charge depends on pH and esterification.
- Uracil is a concrete example of a nitrogenous base, not the structure of all bases.
- Channels follow electrochemical gradients; concentration alone is insufficient for predicting ion flux. Symport/antiport describe directions, not an energy source by themselves.
- Equal-concentration diffusion equilibrium applies to the stated neutral-solute, fixed-volume model; it does not imply that all ions must have equal concentrations across a membrane.
