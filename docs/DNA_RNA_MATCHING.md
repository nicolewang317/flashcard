## Separate scientific clarifications

- Transcription makes RNA from a DNA template. Gene-expression regulation controls when and how much transcription occurs. [NHGRI: Messenger RNA](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/messenger-rna)
- Mutation is a DNA-sequence change. SNP describes variation at a single nucleotide position; neither deletions nor mutations in general are all SNPs. [NHGRI: Mutation](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Mutation), [NHGRI: SNP](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Single-Nucleotide-Polymorphisms-SNPs)
- Replication is semiconservative: each daughter DNA molecule has one parental and one new strand.
- Translation reads mRNA codons at the ribosome to assemble a polypeptide.
